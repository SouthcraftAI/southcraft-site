Custom Artificial Intelligence Solutions for Operational Optimization

Neumáticos Corral, one of Argentina’s leading tire distributors and retailers, partnered with SouthCraft AI to modernize its internal operations through the development of tailored artificial intelligence solutions.

By implementing intelligent systems focused on automation, advanced data analysis, and strategic recommendation engines, the company enhanced operational efficiency, streamlined internal workflows, and strengthened decision-making capabilities across the organization.

This case illustrates how the adoption of customized AI technologies enables retail companies to evolve toward more agile, data-driven, and scalable management models, generating sustainable competitive advantages in highly demanding commercial environments.

Video Games & Interactive Media

Video Games & Interactive Media

AI-Driven Characters for Immersive Real-Time Storytelling

Living Scripts, developed by Widow Games, is an interactive narrative platform where users engage with virtual characters through natural, real-time voice dialogues. SouthCraft AI was responsible for the selection, programming, and implementation of the AI agents that bring these characters to life.

Each character is designed with its own personality layers, emotional states, and behavioral logic that evolve in response to user interactions.

Conversations flow naturally, characters react in emotionally consistent ways, and the narrative shifts based on the user’s decisions and tone of engagement.

This combination of personality modeling, emotional dynamics, and real-time voice interaction sets a new standard for immersive, AI-powered storytelling in video games and interactive media.
